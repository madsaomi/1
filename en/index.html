About learning

Frequently asked questions.

How long does the course take?

General English has 5 levels; the duration depends on your starting level. The SAT course takes 4–5 months. Ask the administrator about other programmes.

Are there lessons in Karakalpak?

Yes. General English in Karakalpak is the same programme with explanations in Karakalpak.

When are the clubs and mock tests held?

The format and frequency of mock tests depend on the programme. Ask the administrator for the dates of Speaking Club, Movie Club and mock tests.

How does IELTS work?

An international English exam for study and work.

The International English Language Testing System confirms your level of English. Universities and employers in many countries accept the result.

Listening — understanding speech, Reading — reading, Writing — writing, Speaking — a conversation with an examiner.

Scores range from 0 to 9. IELTS recommends using results within two years; the receiving organisation sets its own requirements.

Check the teacher, schedule and suitable group before you start.

About IELTS results
What do you need to prepare for the SAT?
98 questions 1600 points

The College Board exam tests English and maths. Half of the total score is English, so the course needs both subjects.

SAT structure — 98 questions
SectionModulesFAQMax. score
Reading & Writing254800
Math244800

Placement test — 50 questions in each subject:

  • English: Upper-Intermediate, at least 40 out of 50.
  • Maths: at least 25 out of 50.

If your English is not strong enough yet, start with General English. If your maths needs work, improve that subject first.

Exam registration is paid separately. Current fees are published on the College Board website.

Check the score validity requirements with your chosen university.
