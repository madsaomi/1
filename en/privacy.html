This Privacy and Personal Data Processing Policy (hereinafter the «Policy») sets out how personal data of website users, students, applicants, event participants, representatives of organisations and other persons dealing with «Stanford Language Center» LLC are collected, used, stored, transferred and protected.

This Policy has been prepared in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan «On Personal Data», the Law «On Electronic Commerce», the Law «On Consumer Protection» and other legal acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

1. General provisions

1.1. «Stanford Language Center» LLC (hereinafter the «Centre») processes personal data to organise the educational process, provide paid and free services, hold events, register users, accept payments and communicate with students, applicants, event participants and other persons.

1.2. This Policy applies to personal data received by the Centre through the official website, the personal account, electronic forms, email, messengers, payment services, registration forms, applications, contracts, questionnaires, requests and other channels of communication.

1.3. This Policy applies to citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan, foreign citizens, stateless persons, students, applicants, conference participants, representatives of legal entities, parents/legal guardians and other persons whose personal data are processed by the organisation.

1.4. By using the Centre's website, filling in a registration form, sending a request, concluding a contract, taking part in an event or providing personal data to the organisation in another way, the data subject confirms that they have read this Policy and agree to the terms on which their personal data are processed.

2. Terms

2.1. Personal data — information relating to an identified natural person or allowing that person to be identified.

2.2. Data subject — the natural person to whom the personal data relate.

2.3. Processing of personal data — any action with personal data, including collection, recording, systematisation, storage, modification, use, transfer, anonymisation, blocking and destruction.

2.4. User — a person who uses the Centre's website, fills in a form, sends a request or pays for a service.

2.5. Student — a person studying at the Centre.

2.6. Event participant — a person who has registered for or takes part in a conference, seminar, forum, training, master class or other event of the Centre.

3. What personal data are processed

3.1. The Centre may process the following categories of personal data:

  • 3.1.1. surname, first name, patronymic;
  • 3.1.2. date of birth;
  • 3.1.3. citizenship;
  • 3.1.4. sex;
  • 3.1.5. phone number;
  • 3.1.6. email address;
  • 3.1.7. residential and/or registration address;
  • 3.1.8. country, city, region;

3.2. The Centre does not collect excessive personal data unrelated to the purposes of processing.

3.3. When services are paid through a payment partner, bank card data are processed by that payment partner, bank or payment organisation. The Centre does not store full bank card data unless such data actually come into the Centre's possession.

4. Purposes of processing personal data

4.1. The Centre processes personal data for the following purposes:

  • 4.1.1. receiving and reviewing applications from learners;
  • 4.1.2. organising admission, studies, academic support and administrative services for students;
  • 4.1.3. registering students, applicants, and participants of conferences and other events;
  • 4.1.4. providing paid additional services;
  • 4.1.5. registering for IELTS exams through official partners;
  • 4.1.6. receiving, identifying and confirming payments;
  • 4.1.7. accounting, tax, statistical, financial and internal record-keeping;
  • 4.1.8. sending notifications, letters, messages, reminders and other information related to studies, services, events and the centre's activities;
  • 4.1.9. organising conferences, seminars, forums, trainings, master classes, round tables and other events;
  • 4.1.10. issuing certificates to event participants;
  • 4.1.11. ensuring security, access control and protection of the Centre's property;
  • 4.1.12. keeping photo, video and information materials about the Centre's activities and events;
  • 4.1.13. communicating with parents, legal guardians, representatives of organisations and partners, where applicable;
  • 4.1.14. communicating with state bodies, courts, banks, payment organisations, migration, visa, educational, accreditation and other competent authorities;
  • 4.1.15. complying with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan;
  • 4.1.16. protecting the rights and legitimate interests of the Centre, students, users, event participants and third parties.

5. Legal grounds for processing

5.1. The Centre processes personal data on the following grounds:

  • 5.1.1. consent of the data subject;
  • 5.1.2. the need to conclude and perform a contract with the data subject;
  • 5.1.3. the need to provide a service chosen by the data subject;
  • 5.1.4. fulfilment of the Centre's obligations under the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan;
  • 5.1.5. protection of the rights and legitimate interests of the Centre, the data subject and third parties;
  • 5.1.6. other grounds provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

5.2. Consent to the processing of personal data may be given by ticking a box on the website, filling in a form, sending a request, signing a document, using the personal account, sending data by email or in any other way that confirms consent was given.

5.3. If processing certain categories of data requires separate written or electronic consent under the law, the Centre may request such consent separately.

6. Transfer of personal data to third parties

6.1. The Centre may transfer personal data to third parties to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of processing.

6.2. Such third parties may include:

  • 6.2.1. banks, payment organisations, payment aggregators and other payment partners;
  • 6.2.2. providers of IT services, hosting, cloud services, technical support, educational platforms, CRM, LMS, email/SMS/push notifications;
  • 6.2.3. contractors and partners involved in organising conferences, forums, seminars and other events;
  • 6.2.4. printing houses, suppliers of passes, badges and other products;
  • 6.2.5. courier, postal and logistics organisations;
  • 6.2.6. auditors, consultants, lawyers, accountants and other professional advisers;
  • 6.2.7. state bodies of the Republic of Uzbekistan, courts, law enforcement, tax, migration, educational, accreditation and other competent authorities;
  • 6.2.8. foreign educational, academic, examination, accreditation, ranking and partner organisations, where necessary for studies, participation in programmes, conferences, exams or the provision of services.

6.3. The Centre does not sell personal data to third parties.

6.4. The Centre requires third parties receiving personal data to use them only for the purposes for which they were transferred and to take measures to protect them.

7. Photo and video recording

7.1. Photo and video recording may be carried out by the Centre on its premises and during lessons, conferences, forums, seminars, official meetings, events and other activities.

7.2. General photo and video materials may be used by the Centre for:

  • a) reporting;
  • b) publications on the Centre's website;
  • c) publications on social networks;
  • d) information, presentation and marketing materials;
  • e) news and press releases;
  • f) covering the activities of the Centre and its events.

7.3. If the data subject is the main subject of an individual shoot, the Centre may, where necessary, request separate consent to use such an image.

7.4. A person who does not wish to take part in photo or video recording may inform the Centre or the event organisers. The Centre will take reasonable steps to respect such a request where the format of the event allows.

8. Cookies and technical data

8.1. The Centre's website may use cookies and similar technologies for the website to work correctly, to improve the user experience, to analyse traffic and to ensure security.

8.2. The user can restrict or disable cookies in the browser settings. Some website functions may then not work correctly.

8.3. The Centre may process technical data, including IP address, device type, browser, operating system, date and time of the visit, referral source and actions on the website.

9. Storage of personal data

9.1. Personal data are stored for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of processing, perform contracts, provide services, keep accounting, tax, academic and internal records, protect the Centre's rights and comply with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

9.2. The storage period for certain categories of data may be set by law, the Centre's internal documents, contract terms, academic rules, reporting requirements or the nature of the service provided.

9.3. After the purposes of processing have been achieved or the storage period has expired, personal data are deleted, anonymised or archived in the cases and in the manner provided for by law and the Centre's internal documents.

10. Protection of personal data

10.1. The Centre takes the necessary legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, modification, disclosure, destruction, copying, distribution and other unlawful actions.

10.2. Such measures may include:

  • a) restricting access to personal data;
  • b) appointing responsible persons;
  • c) using passwords, access control systems and other means of protection;
  • d) backups and technical protection of information systems;
  • e) contractual confidentiality obligations of employees and contractors;
  • f) internal rules and procedures for data processing;
  • g) monitoring compliance with confidentiality requirements.

10.3. Access to personal data is granted only to those employees, departments and third parties who need it to perform their official, contractual or legal duties.

11. Rights of the data subject

11.1. The data subject has the right to:

  • 11.1.1. receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • 11.1.2. request clarification, correction or updating of their personal data;
  • 11.1.3. request blocking or deletion of personal data in cases provided for by law;
  • 11.1.4. withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • 11.1.5. send requests, applications and complaints about the processing of personal data;
  • 11.1.6. exercise other rights provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

11.2. To exercise their rights, the data subject may send a request to the Centre's email address: stanfordnukus@gmail.com.

11.3. The Centre may request additional information to confirm the identity of the applicant and to prevent unauthorised access to personal data.

12. Withdrawal of consent to the processing of personal data

12.1. The data subject may withdraw previously given consent in the same form in which it was given or in writing, including as an electronic document.

12.2. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal was received.

12.3. Withdrawal of consent may make it impossible to provide a service, take part in an event, study certain programmes, register for an exam, issue a document, use the personal account or perform a contract, if processing of the relevant data is necessary for these purposes.

12.4. The Centre may continue processing personal data without the subject's consent if such processing is necessary to comply with the law, protect the Centre's rights, keep mandatory records, perform a contract or in other cases provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

13. Personal data of minors

13.1. If the data subject is a minor, their personal data are processed in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

13.2. Where required by law or the Centre's internal rules, consent to the processing of a minor's personal data is given by their parent or legal guardian.

13.3. The parent or legal guardian confirms that the data provided are accurate and that they are authorised to act on behalf of the minor.

14. Processing data of representatives of legal entities

14.1. If a User acts on behalf of a legal entity, the Centre may process the representative's data, including surname, first name, position, organisation, contact phone, email and information necessary to communicate with that organisation.

14.2. The representative of a legal entity confirms that they are authorised to deal with the Centre on behalf of that organisation.

15. Changes to the Policy

15.1. If significant changes are made that affect the purposes, methods or conditions of processing personal data, the Centre will inform users by posting a notice on the official website and/or by other available means.

15.2. A new version of the Policy comes into force when it is published on the Centre's website, unless the new version states otherwise.

15.3. Users are advised to review the current version of the Policy from time to time.
